- ~~**Tiles do not fill a wide or tall terminal**~~ — **pulled into arc 12 (2026-09-06, D62: the renderer holds the sparkline, the bar width and the bands come from the rect, a §4.6 rule with `assert_grows_with_area`).** Was (bug, Matt, 2026-09-06): Reproduced headlessly: `gridwatch shot --size 480x135` (and live in tmux at 600×100, 800×120) draws a correct grid whose tiles are mostly empty — at 480×135 the CPU tile is about a quarter drawn. The layout engine, modes and tiers are fine at 960×270; the fault is three component-internal rules that derive a *drawing* parameter from size the wrong way, where htop (`Meter.c`: graph history sized to the meter width, one sample per column, bars stretch to the column), nvtop (`plot.c`: ring buffer, one sample per column, newest right) and astral-watch (`src/tui.rs`: ratatui `Sparkline` over a 300-sample `VecDeque`, `Percentage`/`Min` panes) all use "the time axis is columns, wider means more history". **(1) Sparklines derive buckets from width**: `htop/view.rs::spark` and `gpu/view.rs::util_spark` ask `resample` for one bucket per column over a fixed span, so past ≈120 columns the 1.5 s cadence leaves every other bucket empty and the line turns into scattered ticks (`resample` has no forward fill).
